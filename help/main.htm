The main window

Everything NDM is doing, at a glance: a toolbar for the common actions, categories on the left and your downloads on the right.

Toolbar

  • Add URL starts a new download from an address.
  • Resume, Stop and Stop All control the selected downloads, or all of them.
  • Delete removes the selected downloads from the list (and, if you choose, their files); Delete Completed clears the finished ones.
  • Options opens the settings and Scheduler the queues. Start Queue and Stop Queue run or stop a queue; the small arrow picks which one.
  • Grabber opens the Site Grabber.

Right-click the toolbar to switch between large and small buttons, pick the Modern or Classic style, or choose which buttons are shown and in what order.

Categories

The pane on the left sorts your downloads by kind (Compressed, Documents, Music, Programs and Video) under All Downloads, Unfinished and Finished. Each category has its own file extensions and its own folder, so every new download lands in the right place by itself.

Right-click a category to add, edit or delete categories. Grabber projects lists your saved Site Grabber projects and Queues your download queues.

Download list

Each row shows the file name, its queue (the Q column), size, status, time left, transfer rate, last try date and description.

  • Double-click a row to open the file, or its progress window while it is downloading.
  • Right-click for more: open the folder, move or rename the file, refresh an expired download address, add it to a queue or see its properties.
  • Right-click the column headers to choose which columns are shown.

Notification area

Closing the main window keeps NDM running in the notification area, where it keeps downloading and taking downloads from your browser. Click its icon to bring the window back, or right-click it to start or stop the queue, switch the speed limiter or exit.

To quit NDM completely, use Tasks → Exit.
