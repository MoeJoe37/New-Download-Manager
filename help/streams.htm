Video and audio streams

When a web page plays video or audio, the browser extension shows a small download panel over the player. It lists the files and streams the page loaded; choose one and NDM saves it.

HLS and DASH

Many sites send video as a playlist (.m3u8 or .mpd) of short pieces. NDM picks the best quality, downloads the pieces over several connections, decrypts AES-128 pieces and joins the video and audio into a single MP4 or WebM file, without any extra program.

Live streams are recorded until you click Stop, and the recording so far is kept.

mms, rtmp and rtsp

These older streaming addresses are recorded with ffmpeg. Install ffmpeg and make sure ffmpeg.exe is on your PATH.

The download panel

Streams protected by DRM can't be downloaded. In Options → General → Edit you choose whether the panel appears over them, which file types and minimum sizes it lists, and on which sites it stays hidden.
