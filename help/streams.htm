Video and audio streams

When a web page plays video or audio, the browser extension shows a small download panel over the player. The panel lists the files and streams the page loaded; click one to download it with NDM.

HLS and DASH

Many sites send video as a playlist (.m3u8 or .mpd) of short pieces. NDM picks the highest quality, downloads the pieces over several connections, decrypts AES-128 pieces, and joins video and audio into one MP4 or WebM file. No other program is needed.

A live stream is recorded until you click Stop; what was recorded up to then is kept.

mms, rtmp and rtsp

NDM records these older stream addresses with ffmpeg, which you install yourself. It has to be on your PATH: ffmpeg.exe on Windows, the ffmpeg package of your distribution on Linux.

YouTube and other video sites

Sites that play videos in their own player (YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, SoundCloud, Bilibili and others) are downloaded with yt-dlp. Choose Downloads → Set yt-dlp once: NDM downloads yt-dlp for your system, checks it against the SHA-256 its release lists, adds its folder to your PATH (so yt-dlp works in a terminal too) and keeps it up to date in the background, looking for a new version once a day. A yt-dlp you put next to NDM yourself is used first. With ffmpeg installed too, NDM gets the best video and audio and joins them into one MP4; without it, the best single file.

Add the page's address with Add URL, or right-click the page in your browser and choose Download this video with New Download Manager. The file is named after the video's title and goes to the Video category.

The download panel

On the General page of Options, the button next to Customize download panels in browsers sets which file types and smallest sizes the panel lists, on which sites it stays hidden, and whether it appears over players that use DRM.

Streams protected by DRM can't be downloaded.
