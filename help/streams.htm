Video and audio streams

When a web page plays video or audio, the browser extension shows a small download panel over the player. The panel lists the files and streams the page loaded; click one to download it with NDM. When the page hasn't loaded a file NDM can take (nothing played yet, or a player that streams in pieces, as YouTube's does), the panel hands the page itself to NDM, which gets the video with yt-dlp: the video doesn't have to play first.

HLS and DASH

Many sites send video as a playlist (.m3u8 or .mpd) of short pieces. NDM picks the highest quality, downloads the pieces over several connections, decrypts AES-128 pieces, and joins video and audio into one MP4 or WebM file. No other program is needed.

A live stream is recorded until you click Stop; what was recorded up to then is kept.

mms, rtmp and rtsp

NDM records these older stream addresses with ffmpeg, which you install yourself. It has to be on your PATH: ffmpeg.exe on Windows, the ffmpeg package of your distribution on Linux.

YouTube and other video sites

Sites that play videos in their own player (YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, SoundCloud, Bilibili and others) are downloaded with yt-dlp. Choose Downloads → Set yt-dlp once: NDM downloads yt-dlp for your system, and Deno, the JavaScript runtime yt-dlp needs for YouTube, checks each against the SHA-256 its release lists, adds its folder to your PATH (so yt-dlp works in a terminal too) and keeps it up to date in the background, looking for a new version once a day. A yt-dlp you put next to NDM yourself is used first. NDM gets the best video and audio and joins them into one file: with ffmpeg when it is installed, else by itself (MP4 or WebM). A site with no separate streams gives its single file.

Add the page's address with Add URL, click Download this video on the player, or right-click the page in your browser and choose Download this video with New Download Manager. If yt-dlp can't get the video, NDM shows its message (for example a video that needs signing in). The file is named after the video's title and goes to the Video category.

The download panel

On the General page of Options, the button next to Customize download panels in browsers sets which file types and smallest sizes the panel lists, on which sites it stays hidden, and whether it appears over players that use DRM.

Right-click the panel to hide it for now, on that site, or everywhere. The extension's own options (right-click the extension's button in the browser's toolbar → Extension options, which opens them in a tab) turn it back on and list the sites it is hidden on; the same menu has Show the download panel over videos.

After the download

Some downloads need work once the data is in: the pieces of a stream joined into one file, a video's picture and sound joined, or the file moved from the temporary folder to a folder on another drive. The progress window's bar then fills again for that step, from 0 to 100 %, with what NDM is doing, its percentage and the time left, and the list shows Processing with its percentage.

When a file's name doesn't say what it is (an address like watch or download.php), NDM names it after the type the server reports and, once it is downloaded, reads the file itself: it gets the right extension and goes to its category (Video, Music, Documents...) and that category's folder.

Streams protected by DRM can't be downloaded.
